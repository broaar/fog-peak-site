Privacy Policy for Bible Fed

Effective date: May 7, 2026

Bible Fed is a Bible reading app published by FogPeak LLC. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may collect and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Bible Fed may collect limited usage and diagnostic information through third-party services such as Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. This may include app usage events, device and app information, crash reports, and general diagnostic data that help us improve the app and fix problems.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to understand how the app is used, improve features, monitor performance, and diagnose crashes or stability issues.

Purchases

Bible Fed offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock premium features. Purchase processing is handled by Apple. We do not receive your full payment information.

Notifications

If you choose to enable notifications, Bible Fed may send local notifications such as reading reminders or daily verse reminders. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

Accounts

Bible Fed does not require you to create an account to use the app.

Third-Party Services

Bible Fed uses third-party services including Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, which may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Your Choices

You can stop all collection by uninstalling the app. You can also control notification permissions through your device settings.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at fogpeakllc@gmail.com.
